— Investigation Tasks —

1. Capability

The audit team confirms that Project ECHO was not breached.
There were no signs of forced entry, no external intrusion attempts, and no system vulnerabilities exploited.
This wasn’t an attack from the outside.
Someone with legitimate access used the system exactly as it was designed to be used.

Your task is to identify who within the organization even has the capability to interact with such critical systems.

2. Communication Patterns

Internal communications were reviewed during the investigation.
Most conversations appear routine — system checks, status updates, and daily operations.
However, a few exchanges stand out.
Some employees were unusually active.
Others mentioned irregularities in the system before the incidents occurred.
Activity alone doesn’t mean intent.
But patterns in communication can reveal more than direct evidence.

Your task is to identify which employees stand out — and more importantly, why.

3. Financial Signals

Financial records were also flagged during the audit.
Several employees show irregular transactions — unusual amounts, flagged entries, and external links.
At first glance, this suggests possible motive.
But not every suspicious trail leads to the truth.
Some anomalies may be distractions… or something else entirely.

Your task is to determine whether these financial patterns actually connect to the incident — or if they mislead the investigation.
